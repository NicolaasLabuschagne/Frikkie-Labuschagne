About

On & off the stage

Frikkie Labuschagne is a Cape Town-based actor, currently in his third and final year at Act Cape Town. A Caucasian South African with mixed heritage tracing back to Paris, France and Norway, he has been studying acting for seven years, working chiefly through the Meisner technique alongside character work, method acting, and animal work.

During his time at ACT he has performed in one-man shows, stage plays, and improv, and has starred in the short films Conviction and Discount Stripper. Outside of ACT, he starred in Kerry Black’s short film Home Sweet Home. Frikkie is especially passionate about voice acting — creating characters and experimenting with different voices to bring them to life — and his goal is to build a career as a versatile, memorable actor while pursuing voice acting professionally.

Away from set and stage, he’s deep into gaming, anime, and fantasy — articulate and knowledgeable enough to be a vocal, well-informed critic of film adaptations that missed the mark. He holds strong moral values, is outspoken against corruption and greed, and is fiercely loyal to the people close to him.
